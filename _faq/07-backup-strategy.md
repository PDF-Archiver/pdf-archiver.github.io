**Important:** PDF Archiver is NOT a backup solution. It is a document organization tool that helps you tag and archive your PDFs, but it does not provide backup functionality.

## Why You Need Backups

Your archived documents are valuable and irreplaceable. Hardware failures, accidental deletions, ransomware, or natural disasters can result in permanent data loss. That's why you should always maintain proper backups of your archive folder.

## Recommended: 3-2-1 Backup Strategy

We recommend following the **3-2-1 backup strategy**:

* **3** copies of your data
* **2** different storage media types
* **1** copy stored off-site (remote location)

### Example Implementation

1. **Original data** - Your PDF archive in iCloud Drive or local storage
2. **Local backup** - Time Machine backup on an external hard drive
3. **Off-site backup** - Cloud backup service or external drive stored at a different location
