---
title: How can I update the untagged Documents?
---

**PDF Archiver** automatically monitors your archive and inbox folders for changes and updates the view in real-time. Manual refresh is typically not necessary.

If you notice that your documents aren't updating properly, try:
* Switching between tabs (Inbox, Search, Statistics, Settings)
* Restarting the app

If you consistently need to manually refresh, please open an [issue]({{site.github_url}}/issues) to report the problem.
