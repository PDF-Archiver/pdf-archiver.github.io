---
title: The list of untagged documents is not updating. What can I do?
---

PDF Archiver watches your inbox and archive folders and updates on its own, so a manual refresh is usually not needed. If something looks stale:

- Switch between the tabs (Search, Inbox, Statistics).
- Restart the app.

If it keeps happening, please [open an issue](https://github.com/PDF-Archiver/PDF-Archiver/issues).
