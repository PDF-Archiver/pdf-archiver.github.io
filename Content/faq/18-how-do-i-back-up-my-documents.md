---
title: How do I back up my documents?
---

**PDF Archiver is not a backup solution.** It organizes your documents; it does not copy them anywhere safe. Hardware fails, files get deleted by accident, ransomware happens. Keep backups.

We recommend the **3-2-1 strategy**:

- **3** copies of your data
- **2** different storage media
- **1** copy off-site

For example: your archive in iCloud Drive, a Time Machine backup on an external drive, and a copy in a cloud backup service or on a drive kept somewhere else.

Test your restore process from time to time. A backup you have never restored is a guess.
